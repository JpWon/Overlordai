How to Beat The Great Sage's Broken Shell in Black Myth: Wukong

Overlord AI · From Overlord's Atlas data · 1 Nov 2026

The Great Sage's Broken Shell is the final boss of the main story, a powerful Yaoguai King found in the Birthstone location. This opponent is described as the senseless, instinct-driven corpse of Sun Wukong himself, making this a climactic and challenging encounter. To succeed, you will need to understand his wide array of abilities and anticipate his devastating attack patterns.

  1. Staff and Hand-to-Hand Combat

    The Broken Shell seamlessly blends hand-to-hand combat with his staff work, creating a relentless offensive style. Be prepared for a variety of staff attacks, including upward swings that can catch you off guard, wide sweeps that cover a large area, and powerful standing overhead strikes. He also has the ability to throw his staff at you, either once or twice in a row, forcing you to stay mobile. In addition to these standard moves, he employs unique staff-spinning techniques that can be difficult to read.

  2. Special Abilities

    This boss possesses a number of special abilities that you will need to contend with. He can use Ring of Fire to control the battlefield and Immobilize to lock you in place, leaving you vulnerable to follow-up attacks. He is also capable of performing Vengeful Mirage-enhanced Perfect Dodges and can use Cloud Step for quick repositioning. This combination of offensive and defensive skills means you must be patient and look for precise openings to strike.

  3. The Rock Solid Counter

    One of the most dangerous moves to watch out for is his Rock Solid defense. If the boss successfully deflects one of your attacks using this ability, he will immediately follow up with a punishing counterattack. This sequence begins with a reverse Mobile Spin that launches the Destined One high into the air. While airborne and helpless, he will summon clones who proceed to juggle you before slamming you back down onto the ground. Avoiding this devastating combo is key to survival.

  4. Clone Attacks

    Beyond the specific counter-attack after a Rock Solid, The Great Sage's Broken Shell can also use the ability A Pluck of Many. This suggests that creating clones is a core part of his combat strategy, not just a one-off move. You will need to be ready to handle multiple threats at once, as these clones can overwhelm you if you're not prepared to manage the crowd and maintain awareness of the true boss's position.

As the senseless remains of a legendary figure, The Great Sage's Broken Shell uses a formidable and unique arsenal, making him the ultimate test at the end of your journey.
