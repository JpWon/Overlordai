How to Beat Yellow Wind Sage in Black Myth: Wukong

Overlord AI · From Overlord's Atlas data · 3 Nov 2026

The Yellow Wind Sage is a powerful Yaoguai King you will face in the Yellow Wind Formation, a distinct location that is part of the Yellow Wind Ridge. This fight is a test of your ability to read a foe's patterns, as the Sage switches up his tactics as the encounter progresses. The battle can be broken down into two main phases, with the boss starting with physical trident attacks before incorporating more supernatural wind-based abilities.

  1. Phase One: Trident Attacks

    At the beginning of the fight, the Yellow Wind Sage relies primarily on his trident. He will use a variety of basic moves, including wide slashes, direct stabs, and overhead strikes. Be prepared for him to mix up the timing and rhythm of these attacks by occasionally leaping into the air. From there, he can come down with powerful aerial slashes and strikes, which can make his assault feel unpredictable. It's crucial to watch his movements closely to anticipate whether an attack is coming from the ground or from above.

  2. The Grab Attack

    The Sage also has a dangerous grab attack that you need to avoid. This move is telegraphed when he starts with a kick aimed at the player. If this initial kick connects, he will follow up by smashing you down onto the ground. The sequence ends with him impaling you with his trident while you are vulnerable. Recognizing the starting kick is the key to evading this entire damaging sequence, so stay alert for any close-range leg movements.

  3. Later Stages: Samadhi Wind

    As the battle continues into its later stages, the Yellow Wind Sage will begin to use his Samadhi Wind powers more frequently and aggressively. His strategy shifts from purely physical attacks to incorporating this wind magic into his offense. You will see his standard trident attacks become enhanced into more powerful gale slashes, giving them new properties and reach. The most significant change is his ability to create a series of wind clones. These clones will join the assault, attacking the player alongside the Sage himself, which greatly complicates the fight.

Success in this encounter relies on surviving his initial physical assault and then adapting to the chaos of his wind-based powers and clones in the final stretch.
