Elden Ring Death Rite Bird Boss Guide

Overlord AI · From Overlord's Atlas data · 26 Oct 2026

The Death Rite Bird is an optional, recurring boss that you can encounter in the world of Elden Ring. These formidable foes only appear during the nighttime and present a greater challenge than the standard deathbirds you may have already faced. Because they are optional, you can choose when and if to engage them, but taking them on requires specific tactics to succeed.

  1. Targeting and Lock-On

    When fighting the Death Rite Bird, deciding where to aim your attacks is a crucial part of the strategy. The boss's head is a weak point and will take additional damage when struck. However, targeting the head can be difficult and risky. The bird moves its head around frequently, which can make it hard to land consistent hits and may disorient your camera view. This rapid movement can also lead to your character being caught at a bad angle when trying to block, rendering your shield ineffective. For a safer and more stable combat experience, it's often better to lock onto the creature's spine instead. Alternatively, you can choose to fight without locking on at all, which can provide more freedom of movement and camera control to keep the entire boss in view.

  2. Melee Positioning

    Positioning is key to survival, especially for melee-focused characters. The safest place to stand is directly behind the Death Rite Bird's legs. From this spot, you are generally out of reach of its most common attacks. Both its dangerous weapon swings and its powerful beak attacks will typically miss a player who is tucked in close behind its legs. Staying in this area allows you to attack its lower body with a reduced risk of taking damage, making it the ideal defensive position to return to between attack patterns. This allows you to focus on dealing damage while minimizing the need to constantly dodge or block its forward-facing assaults.

  3. Creating Critical Attacks

    The Death Rite Bird can be overwhelmed by sustained aggression. If you deal enough damage to the boss in a short amount of time, it can be staggered. A staggered boss will be temporarily incapacitated, often slumping to the ground, which creates a valuable opportunity for a powerful counterattack. When the Death Rite Bird is in this vulnerable state, you can move to its head and execute a Critical Attack. This single, high-damage move can significantly reduce the boss's health and shorten the overall length of the fight. Focusing on consistent damage output is the way to create these crucial openings for a massive blow.

Remember that these bosses are optional and only appear at night, so you can prepare and choose the right time to challenge them.
