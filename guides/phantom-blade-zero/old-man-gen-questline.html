Phantom Blade Zero: Old Man Gen's Questline

Overlord AI · 21 Nov 2026

In the world of Phantom Blade Zero, you will encounter many unique characters with their own stories. One of the first you might meet is Old Man Gen, a figure whose questline, known as "The Journey Home," adds another layer to your adventures in the Village of Lu.

  1. Meeting Old Man Gen

    You will first come across Old Man Gen in the Village of Lu region. He can be found at his hideout, a location that also serves as a checkpoint where you can save your progress at a bell. Speaking with him on the porch of his house is where your involvement in his personal story, a quest called "The Journey Home," begins. This hideout connects to other areas, including the Village of Lu Entrance and the Broken Puppet Pit, making it a central point for exploration in the area.

  2. The Journey Home

    Old Man Gen's questline revolves around a narrative called "The Journey Home." While the full story unfolds as you play, the events are centered on this theme of returning. Following this path eventually leads to you acquiring Old Man Gen's Brass Key, a significant item tied to his story. The quest also presents opportunities to find useful crafting materials, such as Solvent and Polished Forged Metal, in the same areas you explore while helping the old man.

  3. How Overlord helps with this questline

    Overlord AI acts as your live game guidebook to ensure you don't miss out on Old Man Gen's story. It guides you one step at a time and never spoils what's coming up later, letting you experience the story for yourself.

Meeting characters like Old Man Gen is part of what makes the world feel alive, and his quest offers a deeper look into the lives of the people you meet.
