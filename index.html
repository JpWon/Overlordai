Still stuck? Try the free demo or talk to us.

What does the free demo include?

Thirty minutes of runtime with the app open — the real product, not a cut-down one. It reads your screen, speaks out loud and covers the core guide. No card: it ends by stopping, not by charging. One trial per person.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The 30 minutes start without one, and they end by stopping rather than charging. A card is only ever taken when you choose the Subscription or Pro.

Which games does Overlord cover?

For now, single-player games on Steam. There is no support for online multiplayer games. Everything we publish is included in the Subscription, and new maps land in the guide as they are finished.

What platforms are supported?

Linux and Windows natively. Overlord is built directly for your desktop with zero input automation, local neural processing, and native Wayland/X11 and Windows capture pipelines.

Will using Overlord get me banned?

No. It reads pixels — the view a person sitting beside you would have — and nothing else: no game memory, no injection, no input automation. It is built for single-player games on Steam: online multiplayer is not supported at all, and ranked or competitive play is refused by design, which is where third-party tools get players banned.

Is my game data read or sent anywhere?

Read, yes: a frame of your screen and the text of your question, which is how a question about a room it has never seen gets answered. That one call goes over HTTPS to the endpoint behind your own model key. Your audio, the transcript, your voice and your saves never leave the machine.

How many machines can I use it on?

Subscription is one machine. Pro is three, and you can move a seat any time — deactivate a machine, activate another. Licences are re-assignable, because hardware dies.

How do I cancel?

From the account page, any time — monthly or yearly, no lock-in of either kind. Cancelling stops the next charge; access runs to the end of the period you have already paid for.
