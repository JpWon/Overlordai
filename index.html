In plain words

What Overlord AI is.

Overlord AI is an AI guide companion for PC games. It watches your game (with your permission) to show tips, directions and answers on screen and by voice. Think of the strategy guide that used to sit next to the console, rewritten as a voice that plays alongside you.

Who it's for

Players who would rather keep playing than pause for a wiki or a 40-minute video. It's built for single-player games, mostly soulslikes and action RPGs.

Platforms

Windows 10/11 (64-bit), an installer, and Linux (Ubuntu/Debian, a .deb package). Every feature works on both.

What Overlord AI does

  • Only while your game is running, to work out where you are and what's happening.

  • Directions, loot, combos and conversation choices.

  • It speaks answers out loud, and listens when you press F4 and ask.

  • Without changing them, to follow your progress and your button layout.

What it doesn't do

  • It never presses keys, moves the mouse or controls the game.

  • No changes to game memory or game files, and no code injected into the game.

  • It checks each game when it launches and refuses the online multiplayer ones; it's built for single-player games. See Fair play.

  • It only watches while your game is running and shuts off when the game closes. Frames are held in short-term memory and deleted within about 30 seconds, and our servers don't store them. See the Privacy Policy.

What does the free demo include?

Thirty minutes of use with the app open: the real product, not a cut-down one. It watches your screen, speaks out loud and covers the published guides. No card is needed, and it ends by stopping, not by charging. One trial per person.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The 30 minutes start without one, and they end by stopping rather than charging. A card is only ever taken when you choose the Subscription or Pro.

Which games does Overlord cover?

Mostly single-player soulslikes and action RPGs. Mapped and preloaded today: Black Myth: Wukong, Elden Ring, Mortal Shell, Mortal Shell II, Control, Path of Exile and Path of Exile 2. Any other game is researched from public guides the first time you play it, and its map fills in as you go. Overlord is a guidebook, like the strategy guides that used to sit next to the console. It isn't meant to give anyone an edge in competitive online play, and we don't make guides for competitive multiplayer games.

What platforms are supported?

Windows 10/11 (64-bit, an installer) and Linux (Ubuntu and other Debian-based systems, a .deb package). Every feature works on both. You need an Overlord AI account and an internet connection: answers, game research and the voice come from our cloud service.

What do I need to run it?

Overlord runs beside any game your machine already runs and sips the GPU. It needs an internet connection, a microphone is optional, and it runs on Windows or Linux.

Is this cheating?

No. Overlord is a guidebook. It's the walkthrough you'd open on your phone or the strategy guide that used to sit on the shelf next to the game, except it's live: it looks at your screen the way a friend on the couch would and says “you missed some loot over there” instead of you pausing to go look it up. It never touches the game: no memory reads, no injected code, no automated inputs, no modified files. It refuses to run in online multiplayer games, because it isn't meant for playing against other people. If you'd rather find every secret blind, that's a great way to play, and Overlord isn't for that run. It's for the player who paid full price and would otherwise quit at the second boss and never see the back half of the game.

Does Overlord touch the game or play for me?

No. Overlord doesn't read or change game memory, change game files, inject code into the game or press keys for you. It watches your screen, with your permission, the way a friend beside you would, and it reads your game's save and settings files without changing them. Each game sets its own rules about outside help, and you're responsible for following them, so check the terms of the games you play.

Is my screen or voice sent anywhere?

Yes, when Overlord needs them to help you. While screen sharing is on, frames of your screen go to our cloud service, which passes them to an AI model (DeepSeek or Google Gemini) to work out where you are and what is on screen. When you press F4 and speak, that recording goes to Google Gemini to be turned into text. Spoken answers are made by Google Cloud Text-to-Speech. Screen capture only runs while your game is running and shuts off when the game closes. Our service doesn't store your frames or recordings, and on your PC frames are held in short-term memory and deleted within about 30 seconds. Our Privacy Policy lists everything that is collected.

How many machines can I use it on?

Subscription is one machine. Pro is three, and you can move a seat any time — deactivate a machine, activate another. Licences are re-assignable, because hardware dies.

How do I cancel?

Any time, through the link in your receipt email or by emailing support@overlordai.co. Cancelling stops the next charge, and you keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. Refunds are covered in our Refund Policy.

How Overlord Plays Fair
