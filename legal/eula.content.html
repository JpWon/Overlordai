1 · Who this covers, and what the App is

This licence is between you and De Primus Enterprises, registered in the State of Florida, United States ("we", "us", "our"). It covers the Overlord desktop co-pilot (the "App"), the Guide content it speaks from, and the Overlord website (the "Site").

9 · Your data, your saves, your gameplay

We claim no ownership of your saves, screenshots, recordings or gameplay. They are yours, and stay on your machine.

The App captures a frame of your screen into memory and analyses it. It keeps no library of your play: a frame lives only as long as the process that captured it, and the frame-retention limit that would shorten that is a requirement we have not built. The Privacy Policy says so plainly rather than promising a number we cannot back.

What leaves the machine for a live answer is that frame and the text of your question, over HTTPS to the endpoint behind the model key you configured. Your audio, the transcript, the voice and your saves stay here. Anything else that can leave, including the switchable telemetry backend, is set out in the Privacy Policy.

The Privacy Policy governs data: what is collected, why, where it goes, how long it is kept, your rights. Where it and this licence could differ about data, the Privacy Policy wins. Section 12 caps what we owe you; it gives us no right to your saves.
