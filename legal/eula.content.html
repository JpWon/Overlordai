1 · The app

Overlord AI is an AI guide companion for PC games. The app watches your game (with your permission) to show tips, directions and answers on screen and by voice. It runs beside your game on Windows or Linux, and it needs an internet connection and an Overlord AI account, because answers, game research and the voice come from our cloud service.

The app watches the screen you choose to share, only while a game is running, and reads some game save and settings files without changing them. It doesn't read or change game memory, doesn't change game files, doesn't inject code into a game process, and never sends key presses or mouse movements to a game. It's built for single-player games and isn't meant to give anyone an advantage in competitive online play.

You must be at least 13 to accept this licence. If you're under the age of majority where you live, a parent or guardian accepts it for you.
