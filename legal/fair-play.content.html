Fair
play.

Overlordai is a live game guidebook. Our mission is to help players get more out of the games they already own. That only works if the guide plays fair, so here is exactly what Overlord does and doesn't do.

What Overlord does

It helps in two ways.

Consult, the talking tab, is a strategy guide for any game you name, single-player or online: builds, bosses, what to do next. It doesn't look at your screen, and it puts nothing on it while you play.

Live help is for single-player games only. While one is running, Overlord watches your screen, the same view a person sitting beside you would have, and answers out loud when you ask. It also reads your game's save and settings files, without changing them, to know how far you've got. That's the whole mechanism.

What Overlord never does

  • Read or write game memory, or attach to the game's process
  • Inject code, DLLs, or hooks into the game
  • Press keys, move the mouse, or automate any part of play
  • Modify game files, saves, or configs
  • Watch or help live in online games: it checks each game when it launches and stays out of the online ones. You can still ask about them in Consult, the way you'd read a guide

Why this matters

Cheating means one of three things: changing the game, playing it for you, or getting an edge over another person. Overlord does none of them. Strategy guides have been sold alongside games for thirty years and nobody calls a guide cheating; this is that guide, read to you while you play.

Who it's for

The player who buys a game at full price, hits a wall, and would otherwise walk away from it. If you want to discover every secret on your own, play blind and enjoy it. Overlord is for the other run.
