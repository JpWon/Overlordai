Refund
policy.

Version 1.0Last updated 15 October 2026Covers Subscription and Pro

If Overlord AI isn't right for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of your first payment and you'll get your money back in full. Payments and refunds are processed by Paddle, our reseller and merchant of record.

Contents 1 The 14-day refund · 2 Renewals · 3 Free trial and Founder Access · 4 How to ask · 5 Cancelling · 6 Your legal rights · 7 Contact

1 The 14-day refund

For a new Subscription or Pro plan, you can get a full refund of your first payment if you ask within 14 days of it. You don't need to give a reason. When the refund is made, the plan ends and the app stops working for your account.

2 Renewals

Renewal payments aren't refundable, except where the law requires it, if you were charged by mistake, or if a serious problem on our side stopped you from using Overlord AI. Cancel before your renewal date to avoid the next charge. We email you at least 30 days before any price change.

3 Free trial and Founder Access

The free trial and Founder Access (free until 15 November 2026) cost nothing and take no card, so there is nothing to refund.

4 How to ask

  • Through Paddle: use the link in your receipt email, or go to paddle.net, find your order and choose "Request refund".
  • Or email us at support@overlordai.co with the email address you paid with. We reply within two business days and ask Paddle to process the refund.

The money goes back to your original payment method. Depending on your bank, it usually arrives within 5 to 10 business days. Please contact us before filing a chargeback, as it's usually faster.

We may refuse a refund where there's evidence of fraud or refund abuse, such as repeated buy-and-refund cycles.

5 Cancelling

Cancelling and refunds are separate. Cancelling stops future payments, and you keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. You can cancel at any time through your Paddle receipt, at paddle.net, or by emailing us. There is no cancellation fee.

6 Your legal rights

This policy adds to your rights; it doesn't replace them. If you're a consumer in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland, you have a legal right to withdraw from a purchase within 14 days, and other countries give similar rights. Paddle handles these under its refund policy and buyer terms. Where the law gives you more than this policy does, the law wins.
