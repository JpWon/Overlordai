2 What Overlord AI is

Overlord AI is an AI guide companion for PC games. It watches your game (with your permission) to show tips, directions and answers on screen and by voice. It runs on Windows and Linux, and it needs an internet connection, because answers, game research and the voice come from our cloud service. On Windows 10/11, the launcher, Consult and voice run today; in-game coaching (maps, GPS, combos) is Linux-only for now.

  • It watches the screen you choose to share and sends screen frames to our cloud service, which uses AI models to answer. The Privacy Policy explains exactly what is sent and where.
  • It doesn't play for you. It never presses keys, moves the mouse or otherwise controls a game.
  • It doesn't change your games. It doesn't read or change game memory, change game files or inject code into a game process. It reads some game save and settings files without changing them.
  • It isn't meant to give you an advantage in competitive online play. It's built for single-player games, and we don't make guides for competitive multiplayer games.
