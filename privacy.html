What leaves
your machine.

Version 1.0Draft for reviewEffective 29 September 2026Covers the App, the Site and your account

Two things can leave your machine when you play: one frame of your screen with the text of your question, sent to the endpoint behind the model key you supplied, and, when the telemetry bridge is configured, a session record with no screen content in it. Everything else stays where it is, and this page says which parts of that promise are built and which are still requirements.

1 Scope

Overlord is a companion guide that reads your screen and talks to you while you play. This policy covers the App on Windows and Linux, the Site at www.overlordai.co, and your account.

The App is pixel-read only: one captured frame per question, and nothing else. It never reads game memory, never injects code or DLLs, never inspects network traffic and never sends input, and it refuses ranked or competitive multiplayer by design.

Not covered here

  • Your game platform or publisher; Steam and the game have their own policies.
  • The model provider you point your key at, which is a controller in its own right for the frame it receives.
  • Other sites we link to.

3 What the App reads and sends

The read path is a model call

The co-pilot cannot answer about a room it has never seen without looking, so the read path is a model call: one frame and the text of your question, sent over HTTPS to the endpoint behind the model key you supplied. HTTPS is the only protocol it speaks outward.

DeepSeek is the default provider; Google and Groq are the configured fallbacks, contacted only if a key for them is present. The provider you point your key at receives that frame and that question. That is the honest boundary: a frame and a question leave the machine every time you ask.

Nothing else rides along: not your audio, not the transcript, not the synthesised voice, not your save file, not a device identifier.

Your key

Your key stays on your machine: only a masked label, the first three and last four characters, is ever written to a log line or a record. Getting keys into the OS keyring, rather than a file you edit by hand, is written down as a requirement and is not finished. No analytics SDK, crash reporter or third-party measurement runs inside the co-pilot. To remove the call entirely, point the key at an endpoint you control.

4 The session record

When the telemetry bridge is configured, a session record is also written. Its fields are exactly these: app id; game title and slug; detected engine; session length; p95 latency; request counts; masked key labels in use.

It cannot contain screen content and carries no free text. Every free-text field passes a redaction boundary before it can reach an HTTP body or a log line, so a key or file path in an exception message cannot reach the wire.

It answers two questions: is latency inside the p95 4 s bar it is measured against, and is an hour of play cheaper than $0.10. Those are targets; this page publishes no live latency figure.

The gap, stated plainly. An explicit opt-in toggle, with a visible list of every field, is on the roadmap and is not built. Until it ships, treat the session record as on whenever the bridge is configured.

5 What never leaves your machine

  • Your microphone audio. Speech in stays on the machine; there is no ingest of it.
  • The transcript of what you said while playing.
  • The voice. Lines are synthesised on your machine, and pre-rendered lines are cached locally.
  • The guide content you paid for: mapped rooms, loot tables, the objective order, read from local files.
  • Your saves, your install directory and your Steam library as a list. The record names the title you are playing, not what else is installed.

Where those things live

Session state and pre-computed cards live in RAM, in /dev/shm, not on disk, and do not survive a restart. Log lines are session-scoped and pass the same boundary, so a raw key never reaches one. Guide content is a file on disk, read locally.

6 The Site

The Site is a website with no screen access and no knowledge of your play.

If you create an account

There is no waitlist and no mailing list. An account holds your email address and it exists for one reason: a licence key is issued to an account, and the free Demo is a licence. It is created on the sign-up page or by the App, and the record is held in Supabase. The mail that confirms the address or resets a password is sent to you through Proton Mail from privacy@overlordai.co. That is the whole path.

Advertising pixels

Since 5 October 2026 one third-party script runs on these pages: the Reddit Ads pixel (redditstatic.com/ads/pixel.js). We are running a small advertising test on Reddit, and that pixel is how the test is measured. It reports that a page was viewed, which page it was, and the click identifier from the advertisement that brought you here, then sends that to Reddit under a first-party cookie it sets in your browser. It is how we learn which advertisement is worth paying for, and it is what lets us stop showing advertisements to people who already use the App.

What it does not do: it never sees your screen, your licence key, your account or anything the App reads (§3, §5). It does not run inside the App at all — only on these web pages — and the Site works normally without it. If you would rather not be measured, block it the way you would block any ad script, or write to privacy@overlordai.co and we will record the objection against your address.

What the Site does not do

  • No Google Tag Manager, no Google Analytics and no advertising identifiers of our own beyond the Reddit pixel described above. The storefront template arrived with a Google Tag Manager container belonging to its original author, which would have measured our visitors into somebody else's account; it is switched off, and we have not created one of our own.
  • The Site is served by Vercel from a GitHub repository. What it writes to your browser is localStorage for the light or dark theme, plus the Reddit pixel's own cookie described above.

Links that come from an advertisement are tagged with the campaign that paid for them (for example wukong-traffic-3d), so we can count how many visitors each advertisement brought.

No card data anywhere. There is no checkout and no purchase record here. The catalogue is mock data until the licence store exists, so there is nothing to pay with.

7 The free Demo's licence

The Demo is 30 minutes of runtime with the app open. It is the full product: it reads your screen, speaks out loud and covers the published guide. It ends by stopping rather than charging, and no card is taken.

It does need an email, because the licence key is issued to an account. That address, with a machine fingerprint, enforces one trial per person: the trial is a licence issued to a machine and meant to be used once. The fingerprint is derived on your machine for that purpose, and is not a field on the model call in §3.

8 Payments

Checkout is not live. Nothing on the Site can take a payment today, the catalogue is mock data, and the licence store does not exist. When it does, billing will be Stripe, which handles card data: no card number, no security code and no expiry date reaches our systems.

A paid licence holds the email on the account and the licence assignment. Subscription is $14.99/month or $149.99/year, one machine, all published guides. Pro is $39.99/month or $399.99/year, three machines on one account, re-assignable by deactivating one and activating another, because hardware dies.

Cancelling is from the account page, any time, monthly or yearly, with no lock-in. It stops the next charge; access runs to the end of the period you have paid for.

9 Your rights

EEA, UK and Switzerland

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you may access, correct, erase, restrict, port and object to the processing of your personal data, and withdraw consent at any time.

California

Under the CCPA and CPRA you may know, delete, correct and port your personal information, and opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. The only measurement on the Site is the Reddit advertising pixel described in §6: it reports that a page was viewed and which advertisement brought you here, and we do not use it to profile you or to share anything with anyone.

To exercise any of these, write to privacy@overlordai.co. We may need to verify your identity, and we will respond inside the timeframe the law allows.

What a request can actually reach today. Most of what the App handles never reaches us: it is on your machine or at the endpoint behind your key. A request reaches your account email and any licence record. It cannot recall a frame from a model provider.

10 Retention

A captured frame lives as long as the process that took it. A retention limit, where older frames do not survive, is written down as a requirement and is not built. Until it is, that is the whole truth: no limit is enforced today, and we will name the number when one exists — a number we cannot back is worse than the gap it hides.

The session record lives as long as the telemetry it is written to.

An account email is kept while the account exists, and removed when you ask for it. A licence record is kept while the licence is active, and afterwards only to honour it or meet a tax obligation.

12 International transfers

The services in §11 are cloud services and may process data in a country other than yours, including the United States. The model endpoint you configure decides where a frame is processed, and that choice is yours.

The transfer mechanism for each destination is an open decision, not a settled one. De Primus Enterprises is registered in the State of Florida, United States, and the safeguard that will apply to each transfer, whether standard contractual clauses or an adequacy route, is to be named here before launch. We will not claim a transfer ground we have not put in place.

13 Security

What the design does: every outbound call is HTTPS, the only protocol the co-pilot speaks. Session state and pre-computed cards live in RAM, not on disk. Log lines pass a redaction boundary, and only a masked key label, the first three and last four characters, ever reaches one.

Not finished

Getting keys into the OS keyring, rather than a file you edit by hand, is a requirement that has not shipped; until it does, your key's safety depends partly on keeping that file out of a backup or a screenshot.

No certification claim is made here. There is no SOC 2 report, no ISO 27001 certificate and no HIPAA claim in this policy or the product, and there will not be one until an audit says there is. A frame you send is protected by the transport and the provider you chose, and by nothing we have certified.

14 Children

The App is for players old enough to buy and run the games it guides, and is not directed at children. It is not offered to anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under that age; if you believe a child has sent us an address through an account or the Demo, write to privacy@overlordai.co and we will remove it.

The age floor is 13, and the games are not. Single-player titles carry their own ratings, up to 18 in some territories. An account at 13 is not permission to play: the guide is sold for a game the player must already be old enough to buy, and the account age is not the play age.

15 Your switches

The switch that exists today is the process itself. Stopping it stops the screen reads, the model calls and the speech together, because there is no queue that outlives it.

Not built yet

  • A visible "what I just sent" panel, showing the last frame that went out.
  • A single kill switch that stops polling, stops speech and blanks the card.
  • The session-record opt-in toggle described in §4.

All three are requirements with a written definition of done, and none has shipped.

16 Changes to this policy

This is Version 1.0, effective 29 September 2026. Two facts in it are still open — the postal notices address and the frame-retention limit — and each stays marked in place below rather than being filled with a guess. When either is settled the version number and the effective date move with it, and a material change to what leaves your machine gets notice on the Site rather than a silent edit.

The data path is the thing to watch. If a feature ships that changes §3 or §4, the "what I just sent" panel, the frame-retention limit or the session-record toggle, this page is updated in the same change, not afterwards.
